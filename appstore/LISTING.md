## Description
Grab your phone. Grab your friends. Get up and play.

Motion Club turns your iPhone into a golf club and a tennis racket. Put the game on your TV, swing your phone to hit the shot, and play together in any room.

**YOUR PHONE IS THE CONTROLLER**
No extra controllers to buy or charge. Swing to hit, drag to aim, flick through your clubs, all from your iPhone.

**GET UP. GET MOVING.**
Golf takes a real swing. In tennis, step side to side to chase the ball, then serve, smash and rally.

**PLAY WITH YOUR FRIENDS**
- Pass the phone: 2-4 players on one phone
- Quick match online or play with friends
- Nearby lobby on the same Wi-Fi

**BIG SCREEN, ANY ROOM**
Send the game to your TV with AirPlay or a cable while your phone stays in your hand as the controller.

**GOLF AND TENNIS, EASY TO LEARN**
- Golf: coastal links, crater, snow, mesa and volcano courses, with wind, club choice and a shot map
- Tennis: resort, sky tower and volcano venues
- Make it yours in the Locker, wave with Emotes, win matches and level up

Motion Club is in beta. Feedback welcome in the app.
