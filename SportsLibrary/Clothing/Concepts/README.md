## Design direction

Coordinated navy, teal, ivory, sand and coral. Front and rear color blocking makes the outfits readable from gameplay cameras. Boxing includes a first-person glove/cuff inset. Color choices are examples, not gender restrictions or locked palettes.

## Future modular garment breakdown

- Tops: polo, technical tee, sleeveless collared top, camp-collar bowling shirt, zip-neck jersey, boxing singlet.
- Bottoms: court shorts, skort with inner shorts, tailored shorts, straight trousers, relaxed trousers, boxing trunks.
- Footwear: court shoes, golf shoes, bowling shoes, boxing boots.
- Accessories: cap, visor, wristbands, belt; boxing gloves remain separate equipment.
- Color regions: base fabric, secondary panels, piping/trim, footwear upper/sole and accessory accents. Swatches illustrate direction; actual material masks have not been authored.

These are raster mockups, not 3D garments or textures. No Blender scene or existing character was changed. Generated details may differ slightly between front/back views; resolve seams, coverage and color zones during modeling. Ignore any tiny incidental pseudo-markings when creating the final unbranded garments.

After selecting looks: model separate garments on the actual base bodies, preserve the shared rig, skin and test them through sport poses, prevent clipping, and export colorable material regions. Clothing customization is not implemented by these images alone.
