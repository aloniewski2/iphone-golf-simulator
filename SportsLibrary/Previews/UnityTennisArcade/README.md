# Unity tennis — arcade motion revision

- [Male preview](male-tennis-arcade-60fps.mp4)
- [Female preview](female-tennis-arcade-60fps.mp4)

Actual Unity scene renders: 720×480, 480 frames, 8 seconds at 60 fps each. Deterministic movement and injected ball-contact fixtures exercise the real collision path, including forehand and backhand. These are not physical-phone sessions or measured real-time device benchmarks.

Shows faster lateral movement, exaggerated floating-hand strokes, racket trails, a higher behind-baseline camera, and corrected resort landscaping/export. Motion and camera were informed by frame-by-frame review of https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vl4kjeBLrrY, not copied animation data. The scene remains a singles rally prototype, not a finished match mode.

Unity 6000.3.24f1 verification: 61 EditMode and 4 PlayMode tests passed.
