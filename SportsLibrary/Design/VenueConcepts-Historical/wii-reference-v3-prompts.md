## Bowling prompt
Use case: stylized-concept. Create a new high-quality 16:9 3D game environment concept for our immersive, fun resort sports game. Input image 1 is an actual Wii Sports bowling screenshot: use it as the PRIMARY reference for gameplay camera composition, lane perspective and simple color blocking, not as an edit target. Produce ENVIRONMENT ONLY: remove characters, balls in foreground, UI, text, logos, watermarks. Match screenshot's low rear-player camera from the approach looking perfectly straight down one central lane, with parallel neighboring lanes. Foul line roughly 65 percent down the frame, long lane converging on centered distant pins, ample approach foreground for a third-person character. Do not use elevated architectural or diagonal camera. Warm light honey maple lanes, dark gutters, muted pale teal/sage pinsetter fascia, softly lit warm cream upper wall. Ten white red-neck bowling pins arranged in a proper triangular rack at the end of the playable lane, NOT a flat line. Make it a polished contemporary stylized 3D render: smooth appealing geometry, realistic enough wood grain and subtle reflections but colorful clean casual sports-game readability. Resort atmosphere through understated wood and cool aqua accents, peripheral daylight, not decorative clutter. Keep center aiming corridor unobstructed. Wide gameplay camera, not fisheye. No HUD, no arrows, no numbers, no writing, no Nintendo logos. Single full-frame image.

## Boxing prompt
Use case: stylized-concept. Create a new high-quality 16:9 3D game environment concept for our immersive fun resort sports game. Input image 1 is an actual Wii Sports boxing screenshot: use it as the PRIMARY reference for the INSIDE-THE-RING rear-player gameplay camera and simple color blocking, not as an edit target. ENVIRONMENT ONLY: omit all boxers, characters in ring, UI, logos, writing, watermarks and effects. Camera located INSIDE ring at standing player shoulder/eye height, looking straight across the canvas to opposite ropes. This is the background plate behind a close third-person player and opponent, NOT an exterior view of entire ring, NOT an aerial view. Match the source composition: far ropes run horizontally across the middle-upper part of frame, far corner posts near left and right edges, broad unobstructed pale cyan blue canvas fills lower half with room to composite fighters. No near-side rope crossing foreground, no visible exterior ring apron, no center post. Four taut far ropes with red top, white second, blue third, white bottom. White padded far left corner and coral red padded far right corner, original unbranded pads. Darker softly lit tiered spectator seating beyond ropes provides contrast, simplified low-detail audience silhouettes confined to stands. Elevate finish with polished stylized 3D game rendering, rounded inviting geometry, luminous blue canvas, saturated red and cobalt accents, warm arena spotlights, restrained resort leisure atmosphere. Arena remains intimate and readable, not gigantic stadium. No text or emblems on canvas. Single full-frame image.
