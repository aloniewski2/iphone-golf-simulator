# Hero Accessories – Build Rules (2026-10-13)

Accessories: tennis performance cap, golf tour cap, golf glove (left hand, white leather, velcro tab), golf stand bag (legs, strap, clubs with head covers).

1. **Each accessory is its own mesh.** Never part of the head, hand or body mesh. Give it real thickness (cap crown and brim have an inside and an edge; glove has a cuff edge; bag has wall thickness).
2. **Build it alone first**, in a neutral pose, with clean panels and seams (6-panel crown, button on top, sweatband inside, stitched brim; glove palm/back panels and velcro tab; bag with top dividers, strap, folding legs). Then fit it to the heroes.
3. **Attach to a socket:**
   - Caps: head socket.
   - Glove: left hand bone (skinned to the same hand/finger bones as the body, weights copied from the body hand).
   - Bag: back/spine socket, strap over one shoulder.
4. **Caps never merge with or bend the head.** Leave a slight gap (about 1–2 mm) between the inside band and the scalp. The head shape stays exactly as it is.
5. **Caps hide nothing**, except optionally the small area right under the brim if it would poke through.
6. **Future hair slot:** when hair is added, the cap can squash or hide the hair under it. The cap does not change for the hair.
7. **Check before staging:** run the animation/clipping check with every accessory on, both heroes, both kits.
