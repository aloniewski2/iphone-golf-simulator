PHASE 3 — POLISH (as a layer on top of the existing baked keys, scripted and rerunnable)
work/emote-fluid/tools/fluid_pass.py — per clip, per beat, scripted curve edits (not hand-dragged), so the
pass can be re-run after any re-bake. Techniques:
- Chain offsets: shift trailing-bone keys (hips -> spine -> chest -> neck/head; shoulder -> elbow -> wrist)
  by 1–2 frames on small moves, 2–4 on big ones; head leads on looks/turns, head and arms drag on
  body-driven moves (landing, slam, thrust, push-up drive).
- Overshoot + settle into every major pose (5–15% of the move, settle over 3–6 frames, mostly chest/torso).
- Hold-then-snap on the key "money" poses (6–12 frames held, 2–4 frame exit).
- Moving holds: any hold > 0.5 s keeps a chest breath + hip weight drift in the direction of last momentum.
- Anticipation (3–8 frames counter-move) before every big action.
- Bezier/auto-clamped easing everywhere; zero LINEAR keys on body bones; fast stops only on true impacts
  (foot plant, racket hitting court, hands hitting floor in pushups).
- Squash/stretch on jumps, slams and landings via spine/chest/neck TRANSLATION along the bone chain and
  pelvis height, volume-preserving feel, range ~0.88–1.12 of rest length. NO bone scale on any bone that
  parents a socket or prop (hands, forearms, head, feet) — racket, shoes, and future outfits must not distort.
  No new bones.
- Clavicles must actually animate (raise on reach/celebration, drop on settle).
- Fingers: relax/curl overlap on hand moves (1–2 frames behind the wrist), never frozen claws.
- Dance loops: first/last frame match in pose AND velocity.
