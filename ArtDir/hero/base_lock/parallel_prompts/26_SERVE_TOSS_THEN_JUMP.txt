THE NEW TIMELINE (clip seconds)
1. 0.00-0.20 STANCE: unchanged READY.
2. 0.20-0.70 TOSS, FEET DOWN: weight rocks onto the back foot and then forward. The knees stay soft (10-25 deg). The toss arm rises long and straight on the ball path. The racket arm swings down and back, then up, in the existing twist schedule. Toes stay on the court; heels may lift 4 cm at most. No fold-and-pop and no hop.
3. 0.70 RELEASE: toss arm fully extended up, feet down, eyes up on the ball.
4. 0.70-1.3333 LOAD (cartoon anticipation, the squash): the knees bend deep into the trophy pose (max flexion 65-80 deg, at 1.25-1.40). The hips sink and push slightly forward toward the court. The back arches, the shoulders tilt (toss side up), and the toss arm stays up pointing at the ball. Hold a 2-3 frame beat of tension at the bottom.
5. 1.3333-1.58 COCK + EXPLODE: the racket drops behind the back as now. The legs start to extend from 1.50, and the heels come up.
6. 1.58-1.62 TAKE-OFF: both feet leave the court. The back (right) foot pushes last.
7. 1.70 CONTACT (stretch): the body forms one long line from the front toe through the hips to the racket. The hitting arm is extended. The back leg kicks up behind (right heel toward the glutes, cartoon kick), and the front leg reaches down.
8. 1.70-1.85 HANG: the pelvis apex comes just AFTER contact (1.72-1.82), with an ease-in/ease-out hang so the player floats a beat. The toss arm pulls down and in to the chest.
9. LAND (squash and overshoot): the FRONT (left) foot lands first, 2+ frames before the right. The knee absorbs deep (45-60 deg), with a small pelvis overshoot dip and recovery (one bounce, not jelly). The back leg swings through. Then the existing step and follow-through to READY at the end.
Cartoon level: think Mario Tennis. Readable squash before, big stretch at the hit, hang time, and a bouncy landing. Keep it in place and on model, with no limb stretching (bone lengths stay fixed).
