BUILD ALL OF THESE in work/match-anim-set/ as separate clips. One blend, one FBX each, plus a contact sheet.

1. ReadyIdle. Split-step bounce, racket up in front, loop 2.0–2.4 s.
2. SplitToForehand. From ready, land, outside foot pushes, racket unit turn. 0.6 s. No swing.
3. SplitToBackhand. Mirror of 2. 0.6 s.
4. RecoverCenter. After a hit, small steps back to ready. 0.8 s.
5. Forehand. Semi-open. Unit turn, low-to-high, contact out front, finish over the shoulder. Contact ~40% of the clip. 1.2 s.
6. ForehandOpen. Late ball. Stance stays open, more arm, less step. Same contact idea, shorter prep. 1.0 s.
7. ForehandWide. Running reach. Last step is a plant, arm extends, contact later and wider than 5. 1.3 s.
8. ForehandShort. Low ball near the feet. Bend the knees, compact swing, do not spike it. 1.0 s.
9. Backhand. Two hands. Off hand joins before the forward swing. Contact out front, finish high. 1.2 s.
10. BackhandWide. Same two hands, stretched. Off hand still on at contact. 1.3 s.
11. SliceApproach. One-hand slice, racket face slightly closed, step through toward the net, then the clip ends in a split. 1.2 s.
12. Serve. Toss hand up, trophy, contact at full reach, land on the front foot, racket down the opposite side. Contact named. 1.6 s.
13. Return. Compact, split already landed, short backswing, contact out front. Deuce side. 0.9 s.
14. VolleyForehand and VolleyBackhand. Punch, almost no backswing, step through, racket face open. 0.7 s each.
15. Overhead. Pointing arm tracks up, racket in an L, scissor step if the ball is behind, contact high, finish down. 1.2 s.

Do not blend these into one montage. Each clip starts and ends on a pose that can blend to ReadyIdle in under 0.16 s.
