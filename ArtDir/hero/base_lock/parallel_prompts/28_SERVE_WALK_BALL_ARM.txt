THE BUG (Adnan's eye, in the game)
Before a serve, when the player walks LEFT along the baseline, the arm holding the ball goes through the body. It must read clearly as a full arm just carrying a tennis ball at the side or in front: whole arm visible, ball in the fingers, nothing inside the torso, hips, or legs. Walking right, standing, bouncing, and the toss must stay as good as or better than now.

YOU MUST SEE IT HAPPEN FIRST. Build a simulation that reproduces the bug, proves it with numbers and a film, and only then fix it. A fix without a failing BEFORE run is a FAIL.

PHASE 2: THE FIX (after REPRO)
Target look while walking (match heroes): a natural walking carry. The tossing arm hangs at the side, slightly in front of the hip, with the elbow softly bent (about 20-45 deg of flexion) and swinging gently with the walk opposite the front leg. The ball sits in the fingers on the outside or front of the thigh, clearly visible, never against the belly. When the player plants, the arm moves smoothly (about 0.15-0.25 s) up to the ritual hold point, and the bounces start from there.
Likely shape (adapt to what REPRO finds):
- In Match, while walkMode or |walkYaw| > a few degrees or serveWalkSpeed > .2, VisualTossPoint(0) returns a CARRY point built in the walking body's own frame (walkYaw only, NOT serveYaw), read off the Walk clip's tossing hand where possible, with a small offset outward and forward. Blend carry to the ritual hold on the planted/turn-in, using the same eased weight the body turn uses, so the hand never crosses the body during the turn.
- The IK target must never be inside the trunk: before the two-bone solve, push the palm goal out of a trunk capsule set (chest, belly, hips, thighs) plus a ball-radius clearance, and choose the elbow pole outward from the body side so the elbow cannot fold across the chest.
- Lefties mirror it exactly. The opponent's serve walk uses the same code.
Keep: the bounce ritual, wind-up, toss height and path, the serve timing (0.2 / 0.7 / 1.3333 / 1.7), and the strings at contact, all unchanged once planted.
