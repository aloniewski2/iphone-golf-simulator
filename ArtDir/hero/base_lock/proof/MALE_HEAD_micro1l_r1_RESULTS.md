## Method
1. **Start**: ArtDir blend `9bfd788a...` (SHA verified before editing and before install). The Micro1k mandible crest (+ its width trim, `D_k11 - D_k11nc`) is removed first (`data/V_precrest.npy`) so the new edge is built on a clean jaw instead of stacking on the old one; the Micro1k crease undo, Adam's apple / throat and the nape / pelvis chat work are untouched.
2. **Jaw**: arc on the mesh's natural jaw border (n_z = -0.5 iso-line) **extended round the chin to the midline** and up the ramus (chordal Catmull-Rom through 13 control points snapped to the surface), signed Euclidean surface distance s to the arc, crest d(s) = B * exp(-(s-1)^2 / 2 sigma^2) with sigma 5 mm on the jaw side (11 mm at the angle / ramus: no pocket between cheek and crest), 4.5 mm below, B 3.2 mm (1.6 at the angle / ramus, x0.5 at the chin sides), along 8x-smoothed normals, displacement field smoothed 2x, ear clearance 8-24 mm, fade-out at the ramus end, then the Micro1k-style Gaussian inward trim (3 iterations, 0.06 mm residual) so the front outline stays on the plate. **Shading**: the analytic crest slope tilts the custom normals along the across-arc direction (arc binormal, continuous through the crest) so the edge reads at game distance without a taller crest.
3. **Cheek**: Taubin low-pass blend (220 iterations) on the Micro1k cheek footprint (kills the 5-25 mm pad blobs, keeps the broad volume) + a broad smooth deflation 5.0 mm + a buccal hollow 3.0 mm below the cheekbone, along smoothed normals; protection ramps 8-34 mm from the mouth, 6-30 nose, 10-30 eye, 14-36 ear, 3-16 mm above the mandible arc; the silhouette guard never goes below 0.5 (a hard guard made creases), field smoothed 8x.
4. **Lips**: plate-measured plan (`data/lipplan_l2.json`, curves in mm): vertical piecewise-linear warp keyed on the carved lower-border / seam / upper-border loops, forward pads (-y) for the upper lip and seam row, fade-out before the corners and the nose loops; the paint strips (`facem_lips.py`) are cast from the same plan curves so paint edge == carved loop, 48 x 7 grid, skin-vertex poke-through control. LipUp tint in the Unity harness (.60,.38,.31) -> (.72,.48,.37) (plate upper lip ~ 184,122,88; the old dark tint overpowered the taller band).
5. **Stubble example**: per-vertex weight from the signed distance to the mandible arc (full on the jaw / chin, fading up the cheek between 18 and 54 mm, 0.55 dusting under the jaw), a moustache band, protection ramps at lips / nose / eyes / ears; preview shader only.
6. **Install**: vertex-index patch (`merge_l.py`, guard: base identical to the start blend in the head zone and under D) + lip paint rebuild + audit + FBX export (`export_final.py`, Body_M + Face_M, re-import max error 0.0003 mm) under a lock dir with last-moment SHA re-check (`install_l.sh`).
