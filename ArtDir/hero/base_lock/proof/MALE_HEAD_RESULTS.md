## Method
1. **Start**: ArtDir blend `9bfd788a...` (SHA verified before editing and before the first install). The Micro1k mandible crest (+ its width trim, `D_k11 - D_k11nc`) is removed first (`data/V_precrest.npy`) so the new edge is built on a clean jaw instead of stacking on the old one; the Micro1k crease undo, Adam's apple / throat and the nape / pelvis chat work are untouched. r2 was built from the same start with the new parameters and installed as a vertex-index patch over the first Micro1l install (`merge_l.py ... p6 l2`).
2. **Jaw (r2)**: arc = chordal Catmull-Rom through 13 control points snapped to the surface; **the chin part is placed on the chin's inferior border** (support points of the sagittal contours at 70 / 60 deg: (x, y, z) mm (0, -132.5, 1454.3), (10, -133, 1455.1), (20, -130, 1457.5), (27, -126, 1461.2), (34, -122.5, 1466.5), (46, -109, 1472)) and joins the unchanged natural-border arc by |x| 46 mm, so the jawline runs into the chin instead of stopping above it (first Micro1l arc at the chin: z 1462-1468, 6-8 mm higher). Signed Euclidean surface distance s to the arc, crest d(s) = B exp(-(s-1)^2 / 2 sigma^2), B 3.2 mm (1.6 at the angle / ramus, x0.8 round the chin = 2.6 nominal, 2.1-2.4 measured after smoothing / trim), sigma 5 mm on the jaw side (11 mm at the angle / ramus, **11 mm / 10 mm round the chin** because the chin mesh is sparse and a narrow crest made triangular spikes), 4.5 mm below, along 8x-smoothed normals, field smoothed 2x, then the Micro1k-style inward width trim. **Shading**: the analytic crest slope tilts the custom normals along the across-arc direction (arc binormal), the tilt field is smoothed 16 extra iterations on the chin (a coarse-mesh tilt gave small triangular darts at the chin bottom edge).
3. **Cheek**: unchanged from the first Micro1l install (Taubin low-pass + broad smooth deflation 5 mm + buccal hollow 3 mm, protection ramps, silhouette guard floor 0.5).
4. **Lips (r2)**: ABSOLUTE plan from curves traced on the plate (table below): vertical piecewise-linear warp keyed on the carved lower-border / seam / upper-border loops (strength 1.0), forward pads (upper lip 1.9 mm, seam row 0.6 mm, lower lip none), the paint strips (`facem_lips.py`) are cast from the same curves: upper strip seam -> upper border, lower strip seam -> lower border, no taper, thick soft seam ribbon (plan thickness x0.9, rounded ends), 48 x 7 grid with skin-vertex poke-through control. Colours in the Unity harness = the plate's lip / cheek ratios (see the LIPS row).
5. **Stubble example**: unchanged recipe (preview shader only), recomputed on the r2 surface.
6. **Install**: vertex-index patch (`merge_l.py`, guard: base identical to the expected state in the head zone and under D; r2 = rebase over the installed first Micro1l state) + lip paint rebuild + audit + FBX export (`export_final.py`, Body_M + Face_M, re-import max error 0.0003 mm) under a lock dir with last-moment SHA re-check (`install_l.sh p6 l2`).

## Honest notes / limits
1. **Front-view jaw line.** After r2 the visible jaw line (strongest light->dark step per column, lip-avoiding search window) is within 2.6 mm of the plate's on average over |x| <= 60 mm (max 4.6 mm; the first Micro1l install had 6.1 / 11.1 mm: its chin edge sat 5-8 mm above the plate's, which is the 'chin separated from the jawline' look), and its contrast matches the plate's mean (33 vs 34 luminance units). The remaining difference is at |x| 30-40 where our edge is still 2-4 mm above the plate's stubble-pigment edge (we keep the crest on the jaw, not on the neck).
2. **Shading tilt.** Part of the edge definition is lighting-only (custom-normal tilt <= 13.5 deg on 637 vertices, FBX normals = Import so Unity shows it; a re-normalising importer setting would remove it). Geometric crest <= 3.02 mm.
3. **Plates disagree** at the chin by 3-4 mm (front plate: lower, U-shaped; both 3/4 plates: smaller / higher chin). 3/4 jaw + cheek edges within 3 mm: 34 (start) -> 30 of 60; jaw-bottom rows 566-578 mean |error| 1.86 -> 2.43 mm. The r2 chin crest costs the 3/4 chin-bottom contour ~0.25 mm versus the first install's 2.19 mm; front half-width and profile outlines are unchanged or better.
4. **Face_M changed (disclosed):** only the lip + mouth-seam paint strips (re-laid twice: first install, r2); the lips request makes it necessary because the strips must follow the lip surface and the traced plate curves. Unity colours in `HeroBaseMaleFormProof.cs` `FaceSlots`: `Lip` (.76,.52,.43)/.42 -> (.94,.64,.48)/.30, `LipUp` (.60,.38,.31) -> (.757,.493,.333)/.30, `Seam` (.30,.15,.11)/.30 -> (.42,.18,.10)/.28 (rollback copies in `baseline/`). The lip colours are tuned for the default Tan skin (the face paint is not recoloured by the other skin tones - existing limitation).
5. **Not touched (known residuals, not in this brief):** the dark nasolabial line beside one nose wing, expression / smile, ears, eyes, brows, nape / pelvis, neck cage band z 1.40-1.44, the plate's mouth-corner shading. Stubble stays a preview until the skin has UVs / a vertex-colour path.
6. The eye-gate lines are my reading of the proof sheets; the numbers above are supporting measurements, not the gate.
