## What changed

* **Root cause of "tiny palm"** (measured on the FORM2 mesh): the forearm is a 75 mm wide, ~60 mm deep plateau until w = -15 mm, so the flared palm only started there; the palm was 57-63 mm thick for 60 % of its length and then fell to 20 mm in 43 mm (a ~40 deg cliff), which is the dark band at the finger bases in the palmar view; the fingers were 112 mm long over a palm of 104 mm (0.93). The plates (profile / 3/4 views) show palm length >= finger length and a visibly broad palm.
* **Palm** (`hand4.py`): the palm shape functions now live in u = (w - u_w0)/(w_knuckle - u_w0), so the rows keep their u when the web line moves. Web line 60 -> **78 mm** (rows stretch uniformly about w = -40 mm), width gain up to +19 % in the mid / distal palm, finger spacing at the knuckle ring 3.4 -> 6.0 mm (ring width 109 -> 117 mm), palm thickness -2 % proximal / +5 % distal, a **distal palmar pad** (palm-side surface extended up to +13.5 mm at u = 0.9) so the palm runs into the fingers instead of dropping off, and a longer thumb-root morph / web fillet re-tuned (web_amp 0.9, web_k 24 mm, smoothing radius 75 mm) because the first FORM3 candidates folded the web crotch (88-103 deg). The thumb path follows the root-loop centroid (thumb_follow = 1) so the thumb keeps its attachment; its shape is the FORM2 one with the cap rebuilt and the IP bump kept.
* **Fingertips** (`hand4.py` cap_mode = round): cap length = 1.2 x mean (lateral half width, mean half depth) of the last shaft ring, every semi-axis scales with cos(theta) on 9 uniform 9 deg steps, ring centre glides to a pole slightly on the pad side, **no Taubin smoothing on the caps** (FORM2's 45 iterations at weight 1 shrank the first cap rows into a plateau and left the rim crease). The thumb cap uses the same rule (cap length 1.2 x its radius instead of 0.74 x). The DIP bump was raised 0.065 -> 0.080 so the pinky keeps >= 0.6 mm, the thumb IP bump 0.06 -> 0.14.
* **Regression harness**: with default parameters `hand4.py` reproduces the installed FORM2 mesh bit-for-bit (0.0000 mm, identical polygon multiset), so every FORM3 change is intentional. `allmetrics.py` measures FORM2 and FORM3 with the same code.

## Known remaining differences / honest limits

* The plate hands are still chunkier / longer in the profile cameras than FORM3 (`03d_hands_form3_palm_profile_vs_plate.png`), but the arm pose differs there (profile silhouette XOR 9-10 %), so I compared proportions: palm : finger 1.30, breadth ~117 mm vs the ~117 mm I read on the plate. The plates also disagree with each other: the front plate shows the thumb notch, the back plate hides it, so the thumb protrudes in the bald-back overlays (pink) and the two bald-back hand ROIs got 7 % worse (+64 px) while the two bald-front ROIs got 15 % better.
* The plate fingers read slightly fatter and less tapered than ours; I kept the FORM2 taper on purpose (the FORM2 sausage complaint) and only changed the tip shape.
* Tip roundness is limited by the 10-vertex finger ring: at 30 mm zoom the dome still shows faint faceting near the pole (no sharp rim, side contour turn 10 deg). Going smoother would need a 16-20 vertex finger ring, which would change the palm / knuckle topology.
* Pinky : middle finger length is 0.68 (plate ~0.69, adults ~0.72).
* The 10-vertex finger rings and the thumb-web crevice (a narrow pocket between thumb and index base) are the FORM2 construction; only the fillet strength changed.
* Not in Unity yet (see UNITY_REFRESH).
