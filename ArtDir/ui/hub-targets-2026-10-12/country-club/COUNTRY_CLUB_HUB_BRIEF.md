# Country Club Hub — Visual Upgrade Brief

The reference sheets are in this folder. Each sheet shows our current render on the left (BEFORE) and the look we want on the right (AFTER). Make our hub look like the AFTER images.

## Ground rules
- **Don't rebuild the hub.** Keep our island shape, building positions, doors, spawn point, camera angles, heroes, menu flow, and all our real labels and buttons.
- **The AFTER images are a style target, not a blueprint.** Ignore the people, the "Classic Country Club" / "Rosehill" banners, and any made-up text.
- **Tennis and golf areas:** use the exact same camera angle as now. The mode stations, benches and glowing pads stay in their current positions. **Keep our live video screens**: just add a navy label plate with gold text and a thin gold trim.
- **Keep it smooth on iPhone:** use simple, cheap materials, baked or simple lighting, and no heavy effects.
- Don't touch the characters or animations.
- Don't commit or push. Leave changes staged for review.

## Style in one line
A warm, elegant classic country club at golden hour: cream stone, navy slate roofs, gold lettering, green lawns, hedges and roses, cheerful cartoon finish.

## Lighting and finish (everywhere)
- Warm golden-hour sun with soft, long shadows and a light haze in the distance.
- Saturated but clean colors: cream, navy, gold, and lawn green.
- A little life: birds, swaying trees, sparkling fountain water, flags moving in the wind. All of it should be cheap to render.
