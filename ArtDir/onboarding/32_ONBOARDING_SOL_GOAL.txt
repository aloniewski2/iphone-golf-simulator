0. GOAL (one sentence)
======================================================================
A brand-new player opens Party Sports and, without ever being blocked, goes:
  (1) Account (Game Center, or guest) + Character (body, skin, hair, name) ->
  (2) "Connect to a TV or Mac" (live detection, or "Play on this phone for now") ->
  (3) Choose a game (Tennis / Golf cards) ->
  (4) That game's tutorial -> reward (XP + level 2 + white headband) -> that sport's hub.
Returning players resume where they left off; existing players who already finished a tutorial never see onboarding.

9. DESIGN RATIONALE (reference games, briefly)
======================================================================
- Teach by playing, not reading; few coached moments: Clash Royale coaches only unit placement and upgrading, then ramps training difficulty.
  https://medium.com/@Matthewwspencerr/clash-royale-creating-a-sticky-first-time-user-experience-113e17b18f36
  Clash Royale asks for Game Center at first open, then an easy first battle + chest: https://medium.com/design-bootcamp/6-lessons-from-clash-royal-onboarding-40ed13bf2483
- Auto-run, just swing (our forehand-first, sliders-hidden step): Wii Sports Tennis moves the player automatically. https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Wii_Sports/Tennis
- Create your character first, then short scored lessons (our order 1 -> 4): Mario Golf Super Rush starts with Mii creation, then drive/approach/putt lessons, then a Rookie course.
  https://www.ign.com/wikis/mario-golf-super-rush/Tutorial
- Per-sport tutorial only the first time you pick that sport (our "other sport's tutorial on first pick"): Nintendo Switch Sports. https://game8.co/games/Nintendo-Switch-Sports/archives/376681
- One mechanic per mini-challenge, XP even for failing: Mario Tennis Aces Adventure. https://kotaku.com/mario-tennis-aces-adventure-mode-is-a-great-teacher-1826462641
- Mandatory short controls tutorial, then a no-stakes match: Brawl Stars. https://skycoach.gg/blog/brawl-stars/articles/br-beginners-guide
- No-fail first course introducing one obstacle per section: Fall Guys "Welcome to Fall Guys!". https://fallguysultimateknockout.fandom.com/wiki/Welcome_to_Fall_Guys!
- Text-free first level with a hand icon (our golf lesson): What the Golf? https://what-the-wiki.fandom.com/wiki/What_the_Golf%3F_Tutorial
- Simple controls, straight into play: Stumble Guys. https://blakeir.com/how-stumble-guys-hit-225m-downloads
- FTUE: get playing fast, teach through gameplay (https://www.liquidandgrit.com/first-time-user-experience-ftue-toolkit/); per-step tutorial funnel, D0/D1, no sign-up wall
  in session 1 (https://www.devtodev.com/resources/articles/main-metrics-ftue) — our Game Center step is skippable to guest for this reason.
- Permissions in context (camera only when motion is chosen; no ATT): Apple HIG Privacy https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/privacy ;
  Game Center auth early at launch: https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/NetworkingInternet/Conceptual/GameKit_Guide/Users/Users.html
