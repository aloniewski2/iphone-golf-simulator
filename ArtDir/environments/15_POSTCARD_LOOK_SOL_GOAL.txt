PHASE 1: SHARED LIBRARY AND TEXTURE FIXES (blender/scripts libs, golf_look_textures.py, Course/Look PNGs)
Fix in the libraries first, then rebuild every hole on them. Re-run postcard_look_smoke.py and postcard_props_smoke.py after each lib change.
1.1 Mow stripes. Keep stripes subtle: in the Game view, the luminance gap between light and dark bands is at most 12% on the fairway and 8% on the green. The ref greens are bright and nearly uniform. Add a distinct first cut / collar ring that is darker than the fairway.
1.2 Grass life. Keep the round 8 edge fringe and tuft variety. No carpet of identical tufts. Tufts thin out toward the play line and never sit on PLAY_SURFACE where the ball rolls.
1.3 Sway. Every PLANT_ mesh carries R = sway weight (0 at the base, rising toward the tip), G = phase, B = 0, A = 1. Sway amplitude caps match GolfPlantSwayCheck. Plants stay still at the base so they don't slide on the ground.
1.4 Basalt. Rebuild Basalt_C, Basalt_E, Basalt_N, and the column geometry generator. Columns are 5 or 6 sided prisms, 0.6 to 1.8 m across, with varied heights, visible flat tops, and chipped edges. Joints between columns form a connected branching network. Glow (emission) lives only inside the joints and fades with depth, never in repeating ladder rungs or straight full-height streaks.
1.5 Cliff skirts. Add a library function that hangs a cliff from any play edge down to sea or lava level: irregular silhouette (no straight outline run longer than 4 m seen from above), horizontal strata and overhangs for Needle, rugged eroded rock with scrub ledges for Split, column faces for Crater. Vines and moss hang from the top lip on Needle and Split.
1.6 Rocks and stacks. Sea stacks are tall tapered needles (height at least 3x base width) with layered rock, a grass or scrub cap, and a surf ring at the waterline. No 12-vertex rocks. No stacked-box look. No piece stretched more than 2x.
1.7 Surf and sea. Surf is broken white foam that hugs each shoreline unevenly, thicker on exposed points. No continuous white strip. Water stays deep blue right up to the foam, with only a thin lighter shelf.
1.8 Masonry. hole09_masonry.py and the Needle arch use courses of cut stone blocks of varied size, broken tops, gaps, fallen blocks at the base, moss in the joints. No floating blocks.
GATE P1: smoke tests pass; BASALT_JOINTS PASS on a library test scene; PLANT_SWAY_COLORS and PLANT_SWAY_CAPS PASS on a test scene; MOW_BAND_CONTRAST measured and within the caps; a turntable or 3-view render of a sea stack, a cliff skirt section, and a basalt wall section saved to v3/lib/.
