# Hero restart — 5 concept directions (pick 1)

Audience: friends-first iPhone party tennis. Soft plastic / stylized 3D. Modular slots: Hair · Headwear · Face accents · Top · Bottom · Shoes · Racket · optional Trail/FX.

Research takeaways baked in:
- Fall Guys: one strong base body + surface/costume identity; simplify for distance; vinyl-toy feel.
- Silhouette first (3-read: shape → color → detail); racket must separate from body in silhouette.
- Cosmetics sell when the base already feels *important* and slots change identity without breaking readability.

Anti-goals: generic blond Mixamo tennis dude, Roblox default, Fortnite soldier, photoreal.

---

## 1) CROWNED ACE — “Walking trophy”

**Hook:** The character always looks like the person everyone came to beat — playful royalty, not stuffy.

**Silhouette:** Stocky chibi athlete + **small floating crest / crown-visor hybrid** and slightly broader shoulders; racket reads as a scepter-adjacent sports tool.

**Signature:** Soft-gold trim language (piping, shoe soles, racket throat) + crest headwear. Important without armor.

**Personality:** Confident, generous winner energy. Feels *chosen*.

**Cosmetics that sell:** Crest variants (bronze→gold→obsidian seasons), medal sashes, championship bands, court-color polos with embroidered crests, “seed #” patches, trophy-shoe soles, gold-trail FX on perfect hits.

**Why not generic:** Headwear *is* the identity architecture, not an afterthought hat on a stock body.

---

## 2) VINYL STAR — “Collector figure that plays tennis”

**Hook:** Looks like a limited soft-vinyl toy that somehow got onto the court.

**Silhouette:** Extra-rounded limbs, slightly oversized head, short powerful legs (Fall Guys / designer-toy proportions) — funny when diving, adorable in customize.

**Signature:** Seamless soft-plastic materials; joint “toy seam” lines as optional paint; glossy shoe tips.

**Personality:** Charming, collectible, memeable. Feels *precious*.

**Cosmetics that sell:** Paint apps (pearl, candy, chrome), faceplate expressions, stamp/decal skins, boxed “edition” outfits, clear-plastic rare shells, sticker sheets on racket.

**Why not generic:** Material + proportion language is the brand; clothes are editions, not just polos.

---

## 3) RALLY IDOL — “Main-character hair”

**Hook:** Instantly readable from behind by **architectural hair** — the lobby screenshot star.

**Silhouette:** Big, sculpted hair volume (solid opaque locks) + slim-athletic chibi body; hair mass is part of the silhouette like a cape.

**Signature:** One unmissable hair architecture (swept crest / twin fins / soft mohawk volume) that cosmetics recolor and restyle without going transparent.

**Personality:** Charismatic performer; every match feels like a show.

**Cosmetics that sell:** Hair architectures (seasonal silhouettes), visors/bands that *dock* into hair, stage polos, light-stick racket wraps, reaction trails, face paint / freckle packs, “spotlight” perfect-hit FX.

**Why not generic:** Hair is the hero prop — same lesson as Jinx/Kratos silhouette, party-sports soft.

---

## 4) COURT CAPTAIN — “Team heart”

**Hook:** Looks like the friend who organizes the group chat and still cooks on court.

**Silhouette:** Slightly taller torso block, **captain armband volume**, clear shoe mass for grounded stance; racket held with “ready leader” posture language.

**Signature:** Armband / collar stripe / numbered back patch as permanent design grammar (even when swapped).

**Personality:** Warm leader. Feels *important to the squad*.

**Cosmetics that sell:** Team color kits, armband metals, jersey numbers, friendship duo skins, coach jackets, whistle charm (cosmetic), court-side warmups, matching shoe stripes for party lobbies.

**Why not generic:** Social identity baked into slots — cosmetics are “my crew,” not just drip.

---

## 5) NEON SEED — “Mythic underdog”

**Hook:** Soft plastic body, but with a quiet **mythic accent** (eye mark, glow trim, constellation stitching) — feels destined.

**Silhouette:** Classic readable athlete, plus one sharp accent shape (cheek mark / shoulder chevron / ankle rings) that survives LODs.

**Signature:** Controlled neon (never full Fortnite): one accent color max at a time; night-court skins pop.

**Personality:** Quietly legendary — “that player.” Feels *fated*.

**Cosmetics that sell:** Accent mark styles, constellation tops, dusk/dawn material packs, rare glow trails (perfect only), “seed aura” lobby idle, mythic racket throats, season eclipse skins.

**Why not generic:** One mystic mark + restraint = prestige without clutter.

## Recommendation matrix

| Concept        | Importance feel | Cosmetics depth | Phone readability | Build risk |
|----------------|-----------------|-----------------|-------------------|------------|
| Crowned Ace    | Highest         | High            | High              | Medium (crest clip) |
| Vinyl Star     | High (collect)  | Highest         | Highest           | Medium (new proportions) |
| Rally Idol     | High            | Highest (hair)  | High if opaque    | Medium (hair volume) |
| Court Captain  | High (social)   | High            | Highest           | Lowest |
| Neon Seed      | High (prestige) | High            | High if restrained| Low–Med |

**Best default pick for your goals:** **Rally Idol** or **Vinyl Star** (max customize/buy desire).  
**Safest ship:** **Court Captain**.  
**Most “this character matters”:** **Crowned Ace**.
