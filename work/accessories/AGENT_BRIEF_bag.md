## The product (stand/carry bag, real construction)
- Body: tapered rounded-rectangle shell, white with navy panels as in the sheet (navy base boot with a thick rubber base, navy lower side panels, navy front pocket with a zip and a rolled welt, a long navy side zipper pocket with piping), padded rolled top rim with a **4-way divider top** (visible dividers and club grips inside), carry handle, thickness on every shell (a few mm), piping/welts as real small tubes where the sheet shows them, stitched seams via maps (fabric: ripstop/coated nylon = twill 6, leather-ish panels 8, base rubber 4).
- **Clubs**: 3 woods with **knit head covers** (rounded knit shapes with a ribbed cuff — fabric id 9 rib/7 piqué in the maps, navy/white pattern via M.B), 2–3 irons (silver heads visible at the rim), a putter grip, shafts and navy grips sticking up through the dividers. Keep them cheap (head covers ≈ 400–800 tris each).
- **Stand legs**: two hinged legs with a bracket under the rim and rubber feet. Build BOTH poses of the product (`legs_open`, standing as in view 3; `legs_folded` flush against the bag back as in view 2). The fitted/exported state is **folded** (carried on the back).
- **Straps**: padded double shoulder straps (webbing + foam pad, buckle/adjuster, stitched border) as in view 4. In the hero fit only ONE strap is carried over a shoulder — the hero's **right** shoulder; the second strap is stowed flat against the bag back (or omit it in the exported mesh and say which).
- Roles: cloth role `Kit_Bag` (base white, trim navy by M.B), metal/plastic role `Kit_Buckle` (sub = 1: legs, buckles, zip pulls, club shafts, iron heads; the game draws it with a glossy character shader).

## Fit to the heroes (carry pose, behind the back)
- Define a socket **`Back_Bag`**, a fixed-pose child of the bone `UpperChest` (compute its pose like `cap_export.py`/`acc_fit.local_pose_under` do for `Headwear`): origin at the bag's centre of the contact patch on the back, axes aligned with the body (Unity-like local axes x right, y up, z forward). The bag leans back a little (top away from the spine, ≈ 8–12°), bottom ≈ 0.6–0.7 m above the floor, top of rim ≈ shoulder height (z ≈ 1.40), horizontally centred, slightly toward the hero's left so the right strap is the working one.
- Body, legs, clubs: weight 1.0 to the socket bone `Back_Bag` (rigid). **Strap**: smooth weights blending the socket (where it joins the bag) into `UpperChest`/`Chest` (and `RightShoulder` only if needed) along its path: from the bag's top-right, up over the top of the right shoulder (resting on the trapezius, NOT on the upper arm), down the front of the chest on the right, under the right armpit and back to the bag's lower right. No `ArmBlend_*` bones.
- Clearance: every vertex ≥ 6 mm off the body (use `Clearance` against the torso/shoulder region; the strap pad ≥ 4 mm off skin; polo/shirt thickness is ≈ 4 mm more, so aim for ≥ 8 mm above the shirt on the strap), the bag must stay clear of the head (≥ 25 mm to the hair slot volume = the head mesh) and of the arms' swing in Walk/RunForward.
- Pose check: GolfKitHero clips Idle, Walk, RunForward, Drive, IronSwing, Putt, Chip, HalfSwing, Cheer, FistPump, Wave, Emote_*, Intro_* (and the tennis Walk/Run/emotes on MatchHero if you have time). Report per clip the worst bag/strap-vs-body penetration (mm) and show pictures of the worst frames. The bag is only ever shown in the plaza and between shots (walking, idle, waving); swings/emotes with the bag are informational — report them but the pass criterion is Idle/Walk/RunForward/Wave/Cheer/FistPump.
