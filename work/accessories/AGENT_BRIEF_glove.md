# Glove — item brief (`golf_glove`, piece `Kit_Glove_L`, then mirrored `Kit_Glove_R`)

Reference: `ArtDir/hero/targets-2026-10-13/accessories/03_golf_glove_product.jpg` (views: back of hand, palm, side, 3/4, wrist opening, velcro tab open) and the hand/glove in `06_fit_golf_cap_glove_bag.jpg` (white glove, navy tab on the back of the wrist). Read `AGENT_BRIEF_common.md` first.

## The product
White leather left-hand golf glove: back-of-hand panel with a field of perforations (maps: dimple + cavity), padded palm panel with a stitched pad outline, thumb with its own panel/gusset, **fourchettes** (the side gussets between the fingers) as real narrow strips with stitched seams, finger tips with a seam, a **cuff opening with real thickness** (≈1.4 mm shell, a turned/rolled cuff edge and the inner lining visible from the opening), and the **navy velcro tab**: a strap that crosses the back of the wrist, closed over a stitched anchor patch, with a visible hook-and-loop end and a stitched border. Colour via the maps: base = white leather (M.B = 0), tab = trim (M.B = 1; colours are picked at runtime). Leather = fabric id 8.
Shell thickness real (outer + inner + rounded edge). Suggested construction: build the hand-shaped volume from capsule/ellipsoid primitives per finger segment (voxel-remesh them into one smooth glove-shaped solid, or loft cross-sections along the finger chains), cut the cuff plane, thicken, decimate to the tri budget. Panel seams/pad outlines/perforations come from per-vertex distance-to-curve fields (3D polylines you define on the product) baked into the maps — see how `cap_maps.py` does groove/stitch/dimple.

## Fit to the heroes
- Hand rest poses: bone chains `LeftHand`, `Left{Thumb,Index,Middle,Ring,Little}{Proximal,Intermediate,Distal}`, `LeftLowerArm` — positions in `HeroData(sex).rest_pos` (N). Measure finger thickness and length from the body vertices weighted to those bones. The hands are chunky cartoon hands (male big and relaxed-open, female smaller, slightly curled).
- Fit = retarget the product to each hero's hand with a skeleton warp (product skeleton → hero skeleton: per-bone similarity transforms blended with smooth weights), then a clearance pass so the glove surface sits **0.8–1.5 mm off the skin everywhere** and never inside it (the body is not modified; move the glove, smoothly). The glove covers hand + fingers + wrist cuff; the thumb is covered; the cuff ends ≈ 3–4 cm above the wrist bone (`LeftHand`), under where a polo sleeve cuff ends.
- Weights: copy from the body hand (nearest body vertices), restricted to `LeftLowerArm`, `LeftHand` and the 15 finger bones (no ArmBlend), renormalised, smoothed so seams do not tear; max 4 bones/vertex. The cuff blends LowerArm↔Hand like the body does.
- Pose check across the golf clips of `GolfKitHero_Male|Female` (grip poses in Drive/IronSwing/Putt/Chip/HalfSwing, Idle, Walk, RunForward, emotes): no skin poking through the glove (report count of body vertices outside the glove per clip and the worst frames, show a picture of the worst), glove not floating off the hand more than ~3 mm. Fingers curl around a club: the glove must bend with them (check the knuckle/finger-crease weights). If the worst frames fail, fix the weights/fit, don't just report.
- Then mirror to the right hand for `Kit_Glove_R` (mirror geometry, map Left* bones → Right*, same maps, flip the tab so it still sits on the back of the wrist) — second priority, after the left is good.
