What keeps going wrong the targets for the standard

  1. Shirts tear whenever the arms go up or cross the body. Male tennis polo: holes at the chest, armpit, shoulder blade and side seam (serve, smash, every forehand, Jump for Joy, Victory, Shock, Thrust, push-ups). Female tennis polo: the sleeve opens at the armpit in almost every swing. Golf shirts: the hem rides up so a hole or skin shows above the belt, and the sleeve stretches into streaks with the arms overhead.
  2. Hands go into the body and clothes. Any time the hands are low they sink into the shorts, skirt or thighs: Slump hides both hands completely, Stomp and Defeat bury the fists or fingers, and the plaza walk and run swing the hands through the hips on every step. Two-handed grips overlap (backhand, every golf swing). Fingers go into the skull (Shock), and the racket handle or arm passes through the face at the end of the female forehands and return.
  3. Empty hands still hold a racket. Every empty-handed emote (plaza and golf) starts and ends in the tennis grip shape, holding nothing. The old golf emotes (Spike, Scuba, Thrust, intros) start and end in a two-hand club grip with no club.
  4. Hand shapes look wrong. In tennis swings the free hand is a stiff claw or a flat splayed paddle. Fists have the thumb sticking straight up, and the bare right hand folds wrong. The plaza walk uses pinched OK-sign hands. The golf idle, walk and old celebration clips hang stiff, flat, open hands. The serve toss hand is a flat palm instead of cupping the ball. Racket fingers lie open on the handle in the side runs and some finishes.
  5. Trousers and skirts split. The male golf trousers have a see-through slit at the crotch and tear holes in the seat when bending (old golf emotes, push-ups, the old fist pump). Skirts split open in squats and knee lifts. The female golf glove cuff shreds into spikes on the putt and chip.

Clean, nothing circled: Ready (both heroes) and tennis Idle (both). Male golf Idle has no cloth problems, only stiff open hands.
