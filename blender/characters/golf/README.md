# Golf versions of Adnan's Higgsfield characters

Adnan's tennis players (`origin/final-characters`, `SportsLibrary/ArtDirection/Tennis`) redressed
for golf the way he made them: Higgsfield `gpt_image_2_5` edits of his own A-pose design sheets
(same bodies, hair and faceless heads — his FaceAtlas decals supply the faces), then Tripo
(`tripo_h3_1_image_to_3d`, defaults) image → 3D.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `male-golf-apose.png`, `female-golf-apose.png` | the design sheets: cap / visor, navy-trim polo, belted shorts / pleated skort, left-hand glove, saddle golf shoes |
| `target-golf.jpg` | his target art, on an island tee: her with an iron, him at address with the driver |
| `golfer-male.glb`, `golfer-female.glb` | the Tripo models, slimmed from ~1.4M faces / 4K to 60k faces / 1K JPEG textures |
| `turnaround.png` | the full-resolution models from four sides |

Not rigged yet: to play they need fitting to the V4 golf rig like his tennis players (and his
floating-hands standard, SportsLibrary/CHARACTER-STANDARD.md on final-characters).
