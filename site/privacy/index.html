Motion Club Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Motion Club has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell your data.

Summary

Motion Club (“the game”, “we”) turns your iPhone into a golf club and a tennis racket. To do that it reads your phone’s motion sensors and, for tennis steering, the rear camera. Camera images are processed on your phone and are not recorded or uploaded. If you play online, the game stores a display name, your golfer’s look and your scores on our server.

This policy covers the Motion Club app and the game server it connects to. Published by: Adnan Yonathan, 1511 West 7th Street, South Plainfield, NJ.

We do not collect your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone audio or advertising identifier. The game does not ask for those permissions.

How we use information

We use information only to run the game:

  • Turn your phone’s motion and camera tracking into swings, aim and movement.
  • Show your golfer, name and score to the people you play with.
  • Keep your profile, stats and unlocks, and rank rounds on leaderboards.
  • Match you into online rooms and keep scores in sync, including restoring your seat if your connection drops.
  • Limit sign-ups and requests per network address to keep the service stable.
  • Diagnose crashes and tracking problems.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or tracking you across other apps and sites.

Who sees your information

  • Other players. Your display name, golfer look, scores and emotes are visible to people in your room or lobby. Anyone can also view a public profile (name, look and stats) and the leaderboard, which lists your name and best score. Pick a display name that does not identify you.
  • Apple. If you sign in with Game Center, Apple handles sign-in, invitations and matchmaking under Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Hosting. Online play is provided through Apple’s Game Center service. Profiles and leaderboards are stored on a server run on our behalf by a cloud hosting provider, which may see your IP address. This web page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses.
  • Your own devices. When you use a TV or Mac as the display, controller data (phone orientation and rotation, button presses) is sent over your local Wi-Fi. It is not sent to us.
  • Nearby players. Nearby lobbies connect phones directly over your local network and share only your name, look and in-game actions.
  • Legal. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

The game contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and we do not sell or rent your information.

Storage, security and your choices

On your phone. Your profile, stats, unlocks and settings are stored in the app’s local storage. Deleting the app removes them.

On our server. Your online profile and finished rounds are kept while your account exists. Requests use HTTPS, and the server stores only a one-way hash of your sign-in token. To have your online profile and round history deleted, contact us at the address below and we will remove them within 30 days.

Your choices. You can play without signing in to Game Center, deny camera or motion access in iOS Settings, use a name that is not your own, and delete the app at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct or delete your information; contact us to use it.

Children. The game is intended for players aged 13 and older, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it within 30 days.

Changes. If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
